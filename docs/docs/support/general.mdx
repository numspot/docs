# Technical support

Numspot technical support is available to assist you on a daily basis and answer your questions through the following channels:

- Web ticketing interface: https://support.numspot.com ;
- E-mail: support-client@numspot.com ;
- Phone number: +33 1 59 03 50 06.

## Service hours

Support tickets are handled on business days, from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.

The phone service is available on business days, from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., subject to advisor availability.

## Support requests

:::info
To submit a support request and open a ticket through the web, you must first send a support account creation request to the following address: support-client@numspot.com, including the details of the person concerned (last name, first name, e-mail address).
:::

1. Once your account has been created, you will receive an e-mail inviting you to go to the following link: https://support.numspot.com. On the login page, click the "Forgotten password" link.

![login](/img/support/en/create_support_account.png)

2. Enter the e-mail address you provided to us and click the **Send** button. You will then receive an e-mail containing a reset link.

![login2](/img/support/en/create_support_account01.png)

3. Click the link. You will be redirected to the reset page where you can enter a new password. Enter and confirm your new password, then click the **Save the new password** button.

![login3](/img/support/en/create_support_account02.png)

4. After changing your password, click the "back to the login screen" link.

![login4](/img/support/en/create_support_account03.png)

5. Once logged in, you can open a new ticket by providing the following information:

- Request type ;
- Category ;
- Title ;
- Description ;
- `Space ID` ;
- Products ;
- Zone.

6. You can track the progress of your request from the "Tickets" page of your account.
