# About DirectLink

<ActionsSummary />

DirectLink lets you establish a private connection between your network and the Numspot infrastructure. This interconnection relies on dedicated 1 Gb/s or 10 Gb/s links, without going through the public Internet. The applicable service levels are defined in the contractual terms.

:::info
The sponsor must send an email to support-client@numspot.com to request the setup of a DirectLink.
:::

## General information about DirectLink connections
  
> A DirectLink connection is a physical connection between your network and a DirectLink site, which corresponds to a physical location of the Numspot infrastructure. 

> This connection is a 1 Gb/s Ethernet or 10 Gb/s Ethernet optical fiber that gives you direct access to your Cloud resources located in the region corresponding to this site, without going through the Internet. 

> DirectLink Interfaces let you configure these connections by specifying the [VPC (Virtual Private Cloud)](/docs/network/vpc/concepts/) to which you want to connect through your DirectLink connection.

Before requesting a DirectLink connection, make sure that:
    - your Internet service provider or network operator has equipment in the same data center as the targeted DirectLink site;
    - you have a compatible single-mode fiber;
    - you have equipment that supports the BGP protocol.

:::info
Presence of the Internet service provider or network operator on the relevant site:
eu-west-2:
- **PAR1**, Téléhouse (Magny-Les-Hameaux)
- **PAR4**, Equinix (Pantin)
:::

## Advantages

Using DirectLink provides several significant advantages compared with a standard Internet connection:
    - **Dedicated bandwidth**, ensuring consistent performance and speed
    - **Reduced latency** thanks to a direct path between the endpoints
    - **Private and dedicated connection**, providing better data protection
    - **Reduced risk** of communications being intercepted by unauthorized parties
    - **More stable connection**, less prone to congestion issues
    - **Redundancy**: the option to increase availability by deploying two secure accesses
    - Adaptable bandwidth options, of **1 Gb/s** or **10 Gb/s**

## Setup request

Here is the procedure to request a DirectLink connection:

    1. Identify the DirectLink site corresponding to the region where your resources are located:<br/>
    `curl 'https://api.{region}.numspot.com/connectivity/spaces/{spaceId}/readLocations'`<br/>
    -- Replace `{region}` with **eu-west-2** or **cloudgouv-eu-west-1**<br/>
    -- Replace `{spaceId}` with your SpaceID, which you can copy as shown below:<br/><br/>
    ![Copy SpaceID](/img/console/connectivity/directlink/copy_spaceid.png)<br/><br/>

    You should see this message appear at the bottom of the page:<br/><br/>
    ![Copy SpaceID](/img/console/connectivity/directlink/copy_spaceid2.png)
    
    2. Make sure you have a router or an optical fiber in the same data center as the DirectLink site,
    or call on a provider to install it.
    3. If you have not already done so, create a VPC and a [virtual gateway](/docs/connectivity/vpn/virtual-gateway/concepts/) in the desired region, then attach the virtual gateway to the VPC.

    Once these steps have been completed, **contact Numspot support** to start the DirectLink setup process.
