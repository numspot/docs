# Concepts

## Introduction

The **Mistral AI Platform managed service** allows companies to access a
generative artificial intelligence platform (Mistral language models)
hosted in a sovereign cloud environment, without having to manage the
[GPU](/docs/compute/gpu/concept) (Graphics Processing Unit) infrastructure or the deployment of the underlying models.

**Use cases**:

- Conversational assistants and internal agents.
- Text generation, summarization and classification.
- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and exploitation of document data.

## How to obtain the Mistral AI Platform

Unlike the other managed services (PostgreSQL, Kubernetes, Container
Registry) that you enable on your own from the Console, the Mistral AI Platform
managed service is made available **on request, through our sales
team**.

:::info To subscribe to the Mistral AI Platform
Contact our **sales team** to assess your needs and trigger the
provisioning of your instance:

- E-mail: [sales-eu@numspot.com](mailto:sales-eu@numspot.com)
- Or reach out to your **Technical Account Manager (TAM)**.

Specify the **desired URL** for your instance as well as the desired Mistral
**model or models**. Your access credentials will then be transmitted to you in a
secure manner.
:::
