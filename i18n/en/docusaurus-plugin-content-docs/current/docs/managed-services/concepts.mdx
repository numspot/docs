# Managed services

## Introduction to managed services

**Managed services** allow companies to benefit from a complete IT infrastructure, managed by a cloud service provider. These services include databases, application runtime environments, development tools, and much more, without the user having to manage the underlying infrastructure.

### Benefits of managed services
- **Time savings**: No need to manage the infrastructure, the updates, or the maintenance.
- **Scalability**: Resources can be adjusted according to needs.
- **Security**: Service providers ensure security and compliance.
- **Cost**: Reduction of the costs related to infrastructure management.

:::warning
Managed services must be enabled before you start a creation.
Otherwise, a blocking error `Managed Services are not enabled yet in your space` is returned.

To enable your managed services, please contact [Customer Support](/docs/support/general/#support-requests).
:::

## Numspot managed services

### 1. Managed PostgreSQL

**PostgreSQL** is an open-source relational database management system (RDBMS), known for its robustness and its compliance with SQL standards. As a managed service, it allows users to benefit from a PostgreSQL database without having to manage the installation, the configuration, or the maintenance of the server.

**Use cases**:
- Storage and management of structured data.
- Applications requiring ACID transactions (Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability).
- Environments where data compliance and security are critical.

### 2. Managed Kubernetes (K8s)

**Kubernetes** is an open-source platform for automating the deployment, scaling and management of containerized applications. As a managed service, Kubernetes allows companies to deploy and manage containerized applications without having to configure or maintain the [Kubernetes cluster](/docs/managed-services/kubernetes/concepts/).

**Use cases**:
- Deployment and management of containerized applications.
- Environments requiring high availability and scalability.
- Microservices orchestration.

### 3. Managed Container Registry

**Container Registry** is a solution managed by Numspot to store, manage and secure software artifacts, in particular:
- container images (Docker, OCI),
- Helm charts,
- various artifacts (npm, packages, configuration files, etc.).

### 4. Managed Mistral AI Platform

The **Mistral AI Platform** is a generative artificial intelligence platform (Mistral language models) hosted in a sovereign cloud environment. As a managed service, it allows you to use AI models without managing the [GPU](/docs/compute/gpu/concept) infrastructure or the deployment of the models.

Unlike the other managed services, the Mistral AI Platform cannot be enabled on a self-service basis: it is made available **on request through our sales department**. See [Mistral AI Platform - Concepts](/docs/managed-services/mistral-ai-platform/concepts/).

## Interaction between managed services

Managed services can communicate with each other without any additional action on your part.

To make one managed service communicate with another, you must use the "host" of each service. For example, a Kubernetes cluster can connect to a PostgreSQL database using the "host" of the [PostgreSQL cluster](/docs/managed-services/postgresql/concepts/).

To retrieve the "host" of a managed service:
* [Retrieve the status of a PostgreSQL cluster](/docs/managed-services/postgresql/actions/status/)
* [Retrieve the status of a K8s cluster](/docs/managed-services/kubernetes/actions/status/)
* [Retrieve the status of a Container Registry](/docs/managed-services/registry/actions/status/)
