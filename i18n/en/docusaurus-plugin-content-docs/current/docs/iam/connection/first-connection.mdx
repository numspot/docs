# First connection to the Numspot console


**The first-connection steps are the same as when resetting your password.**  
**A recovery link, together with a recovery code, are sent by email.**

## 1. Prerequisites

To perform your first connection, make sure you have the following items:

- **A valid email address** (e.g. `user@domain.com`)
- **A Numspot user account** created for this email address.
    - If required, contact your organization administrator so that they create your user account.
- **The recovery link** and the **recovery code** received by email.
    - If required, contact your administrator, who will be able to resend an activation link (recovery link).
- **A modern, up-to-date web browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).

---

## 2. Connection steps

### Step 1: Open the activation email

- Check your mailbox at the registered address.
- Look for an email with the subject: **"Action required: creation or reset of your Numspot password"**.
- ![image](/img/console/iam/login/en-mail_recovery.png)


- **Do not delete this email** before the procedure is complete.

**Warning**:

- Check that the sender is indeed an official domain (e.g. `@numspot.com`).
- If you have not received the email, check your **junk mail** (spam) or ask your administrator to **resend the link**.

---

### Step 2: Click on the recovery link

- In the email, click on the **recovery link** or copy and paste it into your browser.
- You are redirected to a confirmation page.

**Note:** Why this link?

- It is **unique** and **temporary** (valid for 48h).
- It confirms that the email address belongs to you.

---

### Step 3: Enter the recovery code

- On the activation page, enter the **recovery code** included in the email sent following your administrator's actions (e.g. `984899`).
- ![image](/img/console/iam/login/en-recovery_code.png)
- Click on **Submit**.

---

### Step 4: Set a password

![image](/img/console/iam/login/en-create_Password.png)

- Enter a **new password** that complies with the rules:
    - **16 characters minimum and 72 characters maximum**.
    - At least **1 uppercase letter**, **1 lowercase letter,** **1 number**, **1 special character**.
- Confirm the password by entering it again.

---

### Step 5: Configure multi-factor authentication (MFA) via TOTP

![image](/img/console/iam/login/en-install_TOTP_app.png)

- Choose a TOTP (Time-based One-Time Password) application to use multi-factor authentication (MFA):
    - Recommended **authentication applications**: Google Authenticator, FreeOTP, Microsoft Authenticator.
- Scan the **QR code** or enter the secret key manually.
    - ![image](/img/console/iam/login/en-totp_first_setup.png)
    - *Illustrative screenshot: the QR code and secret key shown are fake (demo data).*
    - ![Two-factor authentication code entry](/img/console/iam/login/en-TOTP_login.png)

**Important**:

- Without MFA, access is blocked after 3 failed attempts.

### Step 6: Access the portal

- You are redirected to the **Numspot console portal**.

![dashboard](/img/console/general/dashboard.png)



Your account is now activated and secured.
