# FAQ - General

We have gathered here the answers to the questions most frequently asked by our users.

## 1. What is Numspot?

<!-- Direct answer first (one sentence), then the context/link. -->

Numspot offers an independent, sovereign and robust cloud. Championing transparency and reversibility, its services are mostly based on Open Source technologies and European solutions. Find out more on the [numspot.com](https://numspot.com) website.

## 2. How can I contact technical support?

<!-- Enumerations follow the standards: lowercase start, semicolons. -->

You can contact technical support through:

- **Email**: support-client@numspot.com (for account creation, incident reports and requests);
- **Web ticketing interface**: [support.numspot.com](https://support.numspot.com) (subject to prior account creation);
- **Phone number**: +33 1 59 03 50 06.
